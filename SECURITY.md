# Security Policy

We appreciate your interest in reporting security vulnerabilities within our project.

## Reporting Vulnerabilities

Please submit all vulnerability reports to the email `contato@sbrdigital.com.br`. Include as much detail as possible, including:

- A clear and concise title for the issue.
- A detailed description of the problem.
- Steps to reproduce the vulnerability.
- Information about the environment where the vulnerability was found.
- Any additional information that may be helpful.

## Triaging Process

Our security team commits to acknowledging receipt of your report within 72 business hours after receiving it. We will confirm receipt of your report and provide an estimate of when we can expect a resolution.

## Disclosure

Public disclosure of a vulnerability will be coordinated with the reporter and will only occur after a solution has been implemented or according to an agreed-upon schedule.

## Rewards

We do not offer a monetary reward, but we acknowledge all responsible reporters in the acknowledgments section of our repository.
